01

Reading the stack

A file sits on the plane of the pattern it implements, inside the cluster belonging to its module. Cluster positions are identical on every plane, so a module is a vertical silo — look straight down a cluster and you see its whole stack.

Links are real imports. A link between neighbouring planes is ordinary flow; a link that jumps a plane is not.
